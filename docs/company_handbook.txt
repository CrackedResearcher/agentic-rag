GOOGLE - INTERNAL COMPANY HANDBOOK (SAMPLE)

ABOUT THIS HANDBOOK
This handbook provides general reference information for employees. It is a
sample document and does not contain complete operational details.

HEADQUARTERS
Google's headquarters, known as the Googleplex, is located in Mountain View,
California. The address is 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043.

MAJOR OFFICE LOCATIONS
Google operates offices in many locations worldwide. Notable offices include:
- Mountain View, California (headquarters)
- New York City, New York
- London, United Kingdom
- Dublin, Ireland
- Zurich, Switzerland

Note: This handbook lists selected office locations only and does not include
headcount figures for individual offices.

WORKING HOURS AND FLEXIBILITY
Google supports a hybrid working model for many roles, combining in-office and
remote work. Specific arrangements vary by team and location.

CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to follow the company's code of conduct, which
emphasizes respect, integrity, and a commitment to users. The full code of
conduct is maintained separately from this handbook.

BENEFITS OVERVIEW
Google offers a range of employee benefits including health coverage,
retirement savings plans, and wellness programs. Detailed benefit information
is provided through the internal benefits portal and is not reproduced here.

TRAVEL AND EXPENSES
Business travel should be booked through the approved travel system. Expense
reimbursement follows the company expense policy.
