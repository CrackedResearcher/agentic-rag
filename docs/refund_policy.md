# Google Store Refund Policy

_Last updated: March 2025_

## Overview

This policy describes how refunds and returns work for hardware purchased
directly from the Google Store. It applies to devices such as Pixel phones,
Pixel Tablets, Pixel Watches, Pixel Buds, and Nest home devices.

## Return window

Customers may return most hardware devices within **15 days** of the delivery
date for a full refund. The device must be returned in its original condition,
including all accessories and original packaging.

Devices that are damaged, modified, or missing components may be subject to a
reduced refund or a restocking fee of up to 15% of the purchase price.

## How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are issued to the **original form of payment**. Once a returned
device is received and inspected, refunds are typically processed within
**3 to 5 business days**. Depending on the customer's bank, it may take an
additional 5 to 10 business days for the funds to appear.

## Non-refundable items

The following are not eligible for refund:

- Digital content and app purchases after download or access
- Subscriptions after the current billing period has begun
- Gift cards
- Devices returned after the 15-day window

## Trade-in devices

If a purchase involved a trade-in, the trade-in value is credited separately and
is not included in the hardware refund. Trade-in devices, once processed, cannot
be returned to the customer.

## Contacting support

Refund requests are initiated through the Google Store order history page or by
contacting Google Store support. Proof of purchase is required for all refunds.
