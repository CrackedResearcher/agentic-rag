GOOGLE / ALPHABET - INVESTOR FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: What are Alphabet's main reporting segments?
A: Alphabet reports three segments: Google Services, Google Cloud, and Other
Bets. Google Services includes Search, YouTube ads, subscriptions, platforms,
and devices. Google Cloud includes Google Cloud Platform and Google Workspace.
Other Bets includes earlier-stage businesses such as Waymo.

Q: What drives most of the company's revenue?
A: Advertising, reported within Google Services, drives the majority of total
revenue. Google Cloud is a smaller but faster-growing contributor.

Q: How is Google Cloud performing?
A: Google Cloud has seen strong, sustained growth driven by demand for AI
infrastructure and enterprise AI solutions. It has become a meaningful and
growing share of total revenue.

Q: What is Alphabet's approach to capital returns?
A: Alphabet returns capital primarily through share repurchases. The company
has also introduced a dividend in recent years. Specific amounts are disclosed
in quarterly and annual filings.

Q: Where can investors find official financial figures?
A: Official figures are published in Alphabet's quarterly reports (Form 10-Q)
and annual report (Form 10-K), filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange
Commission and posted on the company's investor relations website.

Q: Does Alphabet provide formal revenue guidance?
A: Alphabet does not typically issue specific quarterly revenue guidance. It
discusses business trends and investment priorities on its earnings calls
instead.
