# Google Product Overview

This document summarizes Google's major product lines for internal reference.

## Search and Advertising

Google Search remains the company's foundational product, serving billions of
queries daily. Advertising products, including Search ads, display ads, and
YouTube ads, generate the majority of the company's revenue. Advertising is
reported primarily within the Google Services segment.

## Google Cloud

Google Cloud provides infrastructure, platform, and AI services to enterprise
customers. Its offerings include Google Cloud Platform (GCP) for computing and
storage, and Google Workspace for collaboration tools such as Gmail, Docs, and
Meet. Cloud has been one of the fastest-growing parts of the business, driven by
demand for AI infrastructure and enterprise AI solutions.

## Devices and Hardware

The Pixel line includes Pixel phones, tablets, watches, and earbuds. Nest
products cover smart home devices such as thermostats, cameras, and speakers.
Hardware is sold directly through the Google Store and through retail partners.

## Subscriptions and Platforms

This area includes YouTube Premium, YouTube TV, YouTube Music, Google One
storage plans, and the Play Store. Subscription revenue has grown as the company
expands recurring-revenue offerings across its consumer products.

## Other Bets

Other Bets is a collection of earlier-stage businesses operated separately from
Google's core operations. Waymo, focused on autonomous driving, is the most
prominent example. These businesses generate limited revenue today and are
reported separately from the main Google segment.
